Welcome to the **Vibe Coding Best Practices Playbook** — a concise, actionable guide to doing AI‑assisted development safely and effectively.

Why this playbook matters:
- Security, governance, and quality risks grow when teams "vibe code" without structure.
- Clear best practices accelerate delivery while keeping humans in control.
- Consistent guardrails reduce rework, drift, and audit pain.

**Disclaimer:**  
This playbook reflects my personal opinions, based on my experience and research. Please use it as a guide, but always do your own research and adapt recommendations to your specific context and requirements.

- [AI Agent House Rules](agent-house-rules) – How agents operate safely
